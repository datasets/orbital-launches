# Provenance

## launchlog.tsv

- Source: GCAT Orbital Launch Log (derived catalog of orbital launches and their payloads), by Jonathan C. McDowell
- URL: https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/tsv/derived/launchlog.tsv
- Column descriptions: https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/cat/cols.html and https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/launch/lcols.html (launch codes); success scoring: https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/web/intro/success.html
- GCAT release: 1.8.9 (2026 Nov 6), data update 2026 Nov 19 (file header: "# Updated 2026 Nov 19 0315:29"; HTTP Last-Modified: Thu, 19 Nov 2026 03:16:40 GMT)
- Retrieved: 2026-11-22
- Size: 5,310,749 bytes
- sha256: b9617bb528826440016912d0f5bbc50f4ff1340bd3d642df66b2536ea9944449

The file is archived unchanged. The other GCAT files (the full launch list `tsv/launch/launch.tsv`, the object catalogs such as `tsv/cat/satcat.tsv`) were not used and are not archived. A cross-check on 2026-11-22 found that every orbital (O) or deep-space (D) launch with a success or failure code in `tsv/launch/launch.tsv` (7,431 launches) appears in `launchlog.tsv` with the same launch code.
